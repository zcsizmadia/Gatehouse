**Gatehouse will not ship v1.0 with fewer than three maintainers from at least
two organizations.** That is a governance commitment, not an aspiration — see
Phase 4 of the [roadmap](./ROADMAP.md). This table being short is a known,
tracked risk rather than an oversight, and the most useful contribution anyone
can make right now is to help lengthen it.
