# Gatehouse

**The open AI control plane for the enterprise.**

Gatehouse sits between your applications and your LLM providers. It routes the
traffic (the gate), enforces who is allowed to spend what (the guards), and
produces the audit trail and chargeback data your finance and compliance teams
ask for (the toll).

A single NativeAOT binary. SQLite by default. Apache-2.0, with a permanent
commitment that no governance feature will ever move behind a paywall.

> **Status: Phase 0 — Foundations.** Pre-alpha, under active construction.
